# KnowMe / 灵犀 — Public Discovery Profile

**KnowMe（灵犀）** is an Agent-First, Local-First personal AI knowledge operating system created by **NJX**.

**Core proposition:** 把 AI，变成你的能力。 / Turn AI into your capability.

KnowMe explores a continuously growing personal AI workspace that connects voice, long-term memory, knowledge graphs, agents, calendars, tasks, health context, tools and multi-device collaboration.
