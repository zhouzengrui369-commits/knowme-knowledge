## Owner directive

Human Owner time is reserved for final product acceptance of the 1.0 exact candidate and for major product tradeoffs/sensitive authority.

Routine pre-1.0 product validation is delegated to:

```text
Engineering Delivery
→ Local Executor real operation
→ ED personal real operation
→ Candidate Admission
→ Independent Product Experience Review
```

For pre-1.0 Goals/Milestones:

```text
HUMAN_OWNER_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED=NO
```

unless the Human Owner explicitly escalates a particular decision.

For the exact 1.0 final candidate:

```text
HUMAN_OWNER_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED=YES
```

and only the Human Owner may grant it.

Owner exploratory feedback before 1.0 remains valid product input, but it does not make the Human Owner the routine development tester or regression operator.
