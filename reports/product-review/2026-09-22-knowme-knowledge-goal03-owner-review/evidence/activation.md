### Engineering objective

Build the complete bounded voice-to-note first-use product loop. The first screen must read as a product, not a test console: clear purpose, discoverable voice identity setup and recording action, clear recording feedback, transcript control, explicit `整理成笔记`, editable note draft, explicit save/cancel, provenance inspection and recovery.

Engineering must actively discover/fix in-scope defects. Engineering Ready requires final exact-SHA Local Executor real simulator operation with screenshots followed by ED-personal same-SHA operation with independent screenshots. Tests/build/CI alone are insufficient.

After Engineering Ready, Product Governance handles Candidate Admission and Review Eligibility. Independent Product Experience Review performs the full product-experience gate. Human Owner routine testing is not required for this pre-1.0 milestone.
