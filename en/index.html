NJX · KnowMe

Turn AI into your capability.

KnowMe explores an Agent-First, Local-First Personal AI: a continuously growing workspace that connects voice, long-term memory, knowledge graphs, agents, calendar, tasks, health context and multi-device collaboration.

Why KnowMe?

Most AI products still begin with a chat box. KnowMe starts from a different question: what should personal software look like when an agent can understand context, retain memory, use tools and help move knowledge into real action?

Agent First. Local First. Human Always.

What it is exploring

Voice & Capture
Natural capture across voice, files and notes.
Long-term Memory
Context that grows instead of resetting every session.
Knowledge Graphs
Relationships among sources, topics and personal knowledge.
Agent Workspace
Goal- and context-driven collaboration.
Calendar & Tasks
Move knowledge into plans and action.
Health Context
Explore source-backed health context without claiming diagnostic authority.

Current boundary

KnowMe is still under active development. A public page, demo, code artifact, engineering candidate, product review, Human Owner acceptance and a formal release are different states. Public visibility should not be read as a claim that every demonstrated capability is already a released product feature.
